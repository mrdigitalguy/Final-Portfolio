My story

Hi, I'm Kabi Prasad — a UI/UX designer who fell in love with design not because it looked pretty, but because it could actually solve real problems for real people.

I started out sketching interfaces on paper long before I knew what Figma was. Over the past 3.5+ years, I've worked on mobility and AI products — from designing EV rental apps for university campuses to crafting agentic AI interfaces — learning that the best design is the kind users never have to think about.

When I'm not in a product team, you'll find me doing photography, making motion loops for fun, or doodling characters in my sketchbook. I think those hobbies quietly make me a better designer — they keep me curious.
