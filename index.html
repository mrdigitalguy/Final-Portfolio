How I work

Understanding the brief

Every project starts with a clear brief. I make sure I understand the client’s goals, user needs, constraints and brand guidelines before I begin designing.

Research and brainstorming

Research and brainstorming shape my design direction. They help me understand the target audience, market trends and competitors, and explore different ideas.

Design and iterations

I create early concepts, gather feedback and keep refining them. This helps the final design stay simple, user-friendly and effective.

Dev hand-off and testing

I share clear design specifications with developers and review the build closely, testing it to make sure the final experience works smoothly.
